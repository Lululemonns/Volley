FAQ.

What is VOLLEY.?

VOLLEY. is a developing daily hydration powder brand, designed to make hydration convenient and refreshing.

Is VOLLEY. just for athletes?

No. VOLLEY. is for everyday life — work, travel, recreation and everything in between.

How will I use it?

Our planned format is a convenient single-serving powder stick pack that mixes with water. Final directions will be confirmed on the product label.

Which flavours are coming?

We're exploring Strawberry Açaí Lemonade, Lychee Peach, Citrus Lemon–Yuzu–Lime and Mango Passionfruit.
